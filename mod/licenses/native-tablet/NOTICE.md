# Native tablet renderer attribution

QuestPlus ships its own Bedrock geometry reader and Minecraft 26.3 first-person renderer.
No PAL, FP PAL, AzureLib or GeckoLib JAR is bundled or required.

The implementation was informed by the coordinate-transform and arm-mesh conventions of:
- First-Person-PAL-Expansion (MIT): https://github.com/RazorPlay01/First-Person-PAL-Expansion
- AzureLib (MIT): https://github.com/AzureDoom/AzureLib

Their MIT license texts are retained here for proper attribution. These files are
textual acknowledgements only, not packaged animation libraries.
