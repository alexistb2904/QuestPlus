# Use the tablet

Press **J** to open the tablet. Change the key in Minecraft Controls if another mod uses it. You do not need to hold the tablet item to use this shortcut.

## Read a quest

Choose a **category** and select a **quest** to view its objectives, prerequisites and rewards. Pan and zoom the diagram to find other branches. Requirements explain what must be completed before a locked quest can advance.

## Complete and claim

| Action                 | How                                               |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| Track a quest          | Pin it to the HUD                                 |
| Collect rewards        | **Claim** or **Collect All**                      |
| Hand in items or XP    | Use **Submit**                                    |
| Trigger a manual quest | Its action button or `/questplus trigger <quest>` |
| Play together          | Open the **Team** controls                        |
| Adjust visuals         | Tablet settings for size, lighting and 2D mode    |

Completing objectives makes a quest eligible for its configured rewards. Use **Claim** for that quest, or **Collect All** for eligible rewards. A choice reward requires a selection. Inventory space, team rules and reward policies can affect what is available.

**Submit** is explicit. An item turn in consumes accepted items, while an item submission objective can check them without consuming them. XP submission consumes the requested XP. Read the objective before confirming.

## Track your next action

You can pin up to three quests. The HUD is enabled by default and appears when a quest is pinned. Pinned quests stay on the HUD until you unpin them, even after completion or between repeatable quest cycles and tiers. The server validates progress even when the tablet is closed.

![A pinned equipment objective on the Minecraft HUD](/media/hud.webp)

## Hold a physical tablet

The creative Quest tab includes `questplus:tablet`. Holding it shows its carry pose. Right click to open it, then close the menu to return to carrying it. The **J** shortcut and the flat interface remain available.

By default, taking damage closes the tablet. The setting to keep it open on damage is local to your client.
